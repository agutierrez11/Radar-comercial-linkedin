02 · WHAT KIND OF EVENT
Name the participation level — the metric has to match.
02 / 13
The Fall Festival is an INFORM-level event.
Per the IAP2 Spectrum of Public Participation[5], endorsed by the US EPA[6]. Inform-level success is measured by attendance and reach — not by binding community decisions.
LEVEL 1 — OURS
INFORM
Promise: We will keep you informed.
Metric: attendance, reach, repeat families.
Our target: ≥ 1,000 attendees, ≥ 380 of 520 enrolled families represented.
LEVEL 2
CONSULT
Promise: We will listen and report back.
Metric: survey responses, themes captured.
Example: spring curriculum survey — not this event.
LEVEL 3
INVOLVE
Promise: Concerns reflected in alternatives.
Metric: alternatives revised post-input.
Example: playground redesign workshop.
LEVEL 4
COLLABORATE
Promise: Advice + recommendations incorporated.
Metric: co-authored decisions.
Example: PTA + principal joint budget review.
LEVEL 5
EMPOWER
Promise: Decision rests with the community.
Metric: binding vote adopted.
Example: PTA officer election.
WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE BOARD
If we mis-label the event as Empower, attendees expect a binding decision and we fail. INFORM is correct — and that locks the success metric on slide 3.
