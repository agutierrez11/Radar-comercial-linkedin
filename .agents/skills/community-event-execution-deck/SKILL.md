# Run a Community Event like a PTA chair

> The volunteers actually show up Saturday at 8am — every slide answers who, where, with what.

![community-event-execution-deck methodology illustration](https://cdn1.genspark.ai/user-upload-image/slide_agent/v2-catalog-hero/147-pta-community-event-exec.png)

## Why this skill works

- **The treasurer stops asking "but who is signed up?"** A Volunteer Roster Grid lists named individuals per shift with phone numbers — TBD rows are flagged red, so the board sees gaps before the meeting ends.
- **Thursday's board vote happens on the first motion.** The final slide is drafted as a Robert's Rules-style motion with quorum, second, and recorded vote — the chair doesn't have to rewrite it on the spot.
- **You bring the event name, date, and IAP2 level.** Tell the agent what kind of participation you want — Inform, Consult, Involve, Collaborate, Empower — and it returns a deck where the success metric matches.

## Methodology cheat-sheet

**National PTA × IAP2 × Robert's Rules** — the three frameworks every community-event deck needs: a planning model, a participation-level vocabulary, and a board-meeting procedure.

1. **National PTA 7-step model**[^1][^2] — set goal → recruit team → set date → plan logistics → communicate → execute → debrief. National PTA's *Event Planning Guide* and accompanying DOCX templates are the canonical reference. The slide-4 timeline is shaped backwards from these seven steps.
2. **IAP2 Spectrum of Public Participation**[^5][^6] — Inform → Consult → Involve → Collaborate → Empower. Endorsed by the US EPA's public-participation guide. Naming the level on slide 2 prevents the most common error: a Family Fun Night marketed as "Empower" sets a success metric (decision adopted) that the event cannot meet, so the debrief reads as a failure even when the event was a success.
3. **NACo + ICMA civic-engagement guidance**[^3][^4] — National Association of Counties' civic-engagement toolkits and ICMA's "turning public participation into meaningful impact" workflows treat permits / insurance / cleanup as the *gating* items, not afterthoughts. A deck that hides these in appendix gets sent back to the chair.
4. **Robert's Rules of Order**[^7] — the standard for community-board meetings since 1876. Agenda items as motions, time-boxed discussion, recorded decisions. The slide-12 motion is drafted in the exact form the chair will read aloud — including the call for a second and the call to vote — so the board does not have to rewrite it on the fly.
5. **Communicate three times** — PTA-canonical cadence: save-the-date 4 weeks out, reminder 1 week, day-of details day-before[^2]. Each communication names a channel (email, school flyer, group chat, parish bulletin); the slide-8 plan does not lump them as "communications".

## Before / After

### The volunteer slide

**Typical PPT template**

> **Our Team**
> - Maria — Chair
> - Tom — Treasurer
> - Volunteers: TBD
> - Setup: Saturday morning
> - Cleanup: Saturday afternoon

**This skill's rewrite**

> **Volunteer Roster Grid**
>
> | Shift | Time | Named volunteers (phone) | Lead |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Setup | 7:00–8:30 | Maria 415-…, Tom 415-…, +6 confirmed | Maria |
> | Booth | 8:30–11:00 | 12 names confirmed (sheet attached) | Tom |
> | Cleanup | 11:00–12:30 | 6 names confirmed | Priya |
>
> *0 TBD rows. Recruitment closed 2026-09-28.*

### The final slide

**Typical PPT template**

> **Thank you! Questions?**

**This skill's rewrite**

> **Motion to the Board**
>
> *I move that the PTA approve the Fall Festival event plan as
> presented, with a budget cap of $8,400, scheduled 2026-10-19, with
> Maria Chen as event chair. Second?*
>
> *(quorum check · second · discussion · vote · recorded)*[^7]

### The "what kind of event" slide

**Typical PPT template**

> **Event Overview**
> - Fall Festival
> - Saturday October 19
> - For the whole community
> - Fun for all ages
> - Volunteers welcome
> - Rain or shine

**This skill's rewrite**

> **What kind of event is this?**
>
> *IAP2 Spectrum: **Inform**.*[^5]
> Success metric: **300 family attendees + $8K raised for 4th-grade
> science enrichment.**
>
> *(Not Empower. We are not asking the community to decide which
> program gets funded — that decision was made in the September board
> meeting. We are inviting them to a fundraiser. Naming this correctly
> on slide 2 keeps everyone honest about what we are actually
> measuring on slide 11.)*

## What this skill produces

A 12-slide, 1920×1080 execution deck designed to be presented to a PTA / HOA / community board on Thursday night and printed on US Letter for the volunteer crew on Saturday morning. The visual mode is civic: civic-green primary, paper-white background, accent-gold callouts, 16pt minimum body so it prints legibly in a school cafeteria. No stock photo backgrounds behind text. High-contrast for older volunteers reading printouts.
