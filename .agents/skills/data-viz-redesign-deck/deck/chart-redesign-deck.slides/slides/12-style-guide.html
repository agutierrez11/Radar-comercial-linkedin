The rules for next quarter's deck.
Print this. Pin it next to the BI dashboard. Reuse on every chart.
Per-chart checklist
01Question first. Write the one question this chart answers. If you can't, delete the chart.7
02Pick the highest rank. Position > length > angle > area > volume.1,3
03Strip non-data-ink. Borders, gridlines, markers, drop shadows, 3D effects — gone.4,8
04Color last, and sparingly. Gray context · one focal blue · orange only for exceptions.8
05Label in place. Kill the legend. Put the label at the end of the line / bar.5,7
06One scale, one unit. No dual y-axis. Unit in the title. Tabular numerals.9
07Small multiples share scale. Auto-fit per panel hides the comparison you wanted.9
08Annotate the conclusion. One sentence under the chart. The reader gets the takeaway in seconds.5,7
09Tables are first-class. ≤8 rows, fixed columns, ordered → ship a table, not a chart.
