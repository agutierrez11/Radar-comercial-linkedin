09  ·  Cross-Deck Rules (IBCS)
09 / 12
Same scale, same color, same suffix — every chart.
The IBCS notation gives a deck a single grammar. The reader learns it once on slide 04 and reuses it everywhere.9,10
Rule 01
Same scale across small multiples.
If two panels share a metric, they share a y-axis range. Don't auto-fit each panel — that hides the differences the reader came for.
IBCS — uniform scale
Rule 02
Color carries semantic, not identity.
Color means a thing — actual vs plan vs forecast, focal vs context, normal vs exception. It does not mean "this is the Apparel category."

Rule 03
Unit suffix lives in the title.
"$M", "%", "bps", "count" appears once in the chart title — never inside every tick. Tick labels stay bare numbers; tabular numerals only.
IBCS — uniform unit notation
Hard rule   Every chart in the deck must agree with all three rules. A chart that needs an exception gets its exception documented in the appendix — not in a one-off legend.
