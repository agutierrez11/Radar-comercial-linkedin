18 · Style rules going forward
Job: name the rules the team uses to keep the cut deck from regrowing.
Eight rules. Stuck on the wall.
01
Line length 60–80 chars.
Body text holds 10–12 words per line. Atlassian rule [^1].
02
4px base unit grid.
All paddings, gutters, margins are multiples of 4 [^2].
03
Two type weights only.
Regular for body. Semibold for headings and labels. Nothing else.
04
One accent colour.
Slate #3F4A57 only. No warm beige. No gradients.
05
One slide, one job.
The job sentence fits one line. Two sentences = two slides.
06
No pasted screenshots without commentary.
Threads belong in an appendix. The decision belongs on the slide.
07
Atomic levels named on every slide.
Atom · molecule · organism · template · page [^6][^7].
08
CRAP audit before review.
Contrast · Repetition · Alignment · Proximity, four questions [^8][^9].
If a rule is not load-bearing, delete the rule. If a slide is not load-bearing, delete the slide.
