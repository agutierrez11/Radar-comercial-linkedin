The team's printable one-pager.
Atomic Design — five levels [^6][^7]
Name the level on every slide.
Atom
One text, one number, one image. The smallest unit.
Molecule
Two or three atoms working together — label + value + caption.
Organism
A full functional block — a table, a timeline, a slide card.
Template
A reusable layout — the slide card itself, repeated.
Page
The deck as the reader experiences it end-to-end.
CRAP audit — four questions [^8][^9]
Ask all four before review.
Contrast
Does one element clearly carry the slide's job?
Repetition
Do related blocks share an identical molecule?
Alignment
Does every element sit on the 4px grid and a shared edge?
Proximity
Are related things together, and unrelated things apart?
