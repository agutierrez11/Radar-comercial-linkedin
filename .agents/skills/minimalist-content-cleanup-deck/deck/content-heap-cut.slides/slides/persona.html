Job: name the reader so the skill stays usable in 48 hours.
Meet Jules. 47 slides. 48 hours.
The situation
EM at a 200-person SaaS. Quarterly architecture review for the VP. Twelve slides are pasted Slack screenshots with no commentary.
The feedback
“I couldn't get past slide 9.” Jules now has two days, no time to rewrite — only time to cut and merge.
The constraint
No new writing. The output is the input deck minus 30–60% of slides — same content, fewer pages, clearer reading.
Also for
PMs presenting RFCs. Design reviews. Ops reviews. Docs owners moving Confluence pages into slides.
Outcome — Jules ships a 28-slide deck the VP reads to the end.
