04 · Methodology
Job: the six rules every kept slide must pass.
Six rules. No decoration.
01
Atomic Design hierarchy
Every kept slide is named at one of five levels — atom, molecule, organism, template, page. Slides that don't fit are merged or cut.
Frost 2016 [^6][^7]
02
CRAP audit
Every slide passes four questions: Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, Proximity. Failing slides are rewritten or merged.
Williams [^8][^9]
03
Line length 60–80 chars
Body text holds 10–12 words per line. Spacing sits on a 4px base-unit grid; paragraph spacing managed in the type library.
Atlassian [^1][^2][^3]
04
Notion minimalism, cooled
Single display weight, single body weight, generous whitespace. Warm beige is cooled to neutral white plus one slate accent.
Product People [^4][^5]
05
Delete or merge
Pasted screenshots without commentary do not earn a slide. They are merged as a one-line callout in the next slide, or cut.
This skill
06
One slide, one job
The slide's job sentence fits on one line. If it needs two sentences, the slide is actually two slides — split it.
