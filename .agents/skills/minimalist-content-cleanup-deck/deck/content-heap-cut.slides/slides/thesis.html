Job: state why this skill exists in one sentence.
The skill is cutting,
not generating.
Internal decks bloat because authors paste from docs, from Slack, from notebooks — and never delete. In 2026 every author has a co-pilot that paragraphs more text. What scales is the cut.
The professional alternative is documented: Atlassian's spacing and line-length rules [^1][^2], Notion's warm minimalism cooled to neutral white [^4][^5], Brad Frost's atomic hierarchy [^6], and Robin Williams' CRAP audit [^8].
Cut ratio target
≥ 30%
Of the input deck removed or merged. Below 30%, this skill flags that cleanup hasn't actually happened.
