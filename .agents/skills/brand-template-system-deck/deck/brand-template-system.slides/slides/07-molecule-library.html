07 · Molecules
Molecule library · 12 jobs
The smallest useful unit a maker can pick.
Each molecule is bound to one job. Substitution rules are inherited from the atoms it is composed of.
01 · eyebrow label
molecule/eyebrow-label
Mono, caps, accent or mute. Sits 8 px above the headline it labels.
02 · title block
molecule/title-block
Eyebrow + headline. Headline is sentence case, ≤ 8 words, ≤ 2 lines.
03 · kpi card
molecule/kpi-card
One number, one label, one delta. Number uses display/xl. No two-number variant.
04 · citation
molecule/citation
Superscript marker in body; numbered list at slide foot. Always paired.
05 · callout
molecule/callout
Quiet surface with a leading rule. One sentence, max 24 words.
06 · stat strip
molecule/stat-strip
2–4 KPI cards on one horizontal line. Auto-spaced by space/40.
07 · bullet list
molecule/bullet-list
Max 5 items. Marker is the accent rule, not a dot. Sentence case.
08 · footer
molecule/footer
Template tag, page number, hairline rule. Present on every slide.
09 · section number
molecule/section-number
Two-digit, mono, paired with section name. Drives the divider.
10 · source list
molecule/source-list
Numbered list, text/sm, mute color. Pairs 1:1 with citation markers.
11 · image caption
molecule/image-caption
Sits below image at 8 px offset. Caps one line, text/sm.
12 · quote
molecule/quote
Pull-quote, no curly quote marks. Attribution sits below in text/sm.
