Treatment & crop rules
Photography is documentary, never decorative.
An image earns the slide only when it is the subject. No stock decoration, no abstract gradient back-fills, no AI flourishes behind text.
crop/full-bleed
16:9. Subject in lower third. Title overlays at 8 px from edge.
crop/half-panel
Image left, content right. Vertical 1:2. Subject must face inward.
crop/inset
Image sits inside the grid. 8 px caption immediately below.
crop/forbidden
Image as backdrop with text overlay sprawled across. Never.
RULE · Black-and-white treatment by default. Color photography only when color carries the meaning — product, brand, identity.
