02 · The problem
Why the company deck template doesn’t work
Forty-seven layouts produces
forty-seven flavours of off-brand.
Every large company has a brand PPT template. Almost none survive contact with their own employees. The fix isn’t more layouts — it’s fewer, smaller decisions.

A system that gives PMs forty-seven layouts produces forty-seven flavours of off-brand. A system that gives them six organisms made of twelve molecules made of eighteen atoms produces only on-brand decks.

This is the explicit thesis of Brad Frost’s Atomic Design,[1][5] and the operating reality behind every working public design system — Atlassian, Carbon, Material.[4]
