# Art-direct an Annual Report like a Magazine art director

> One stance, two type weights, one photographer — the rest is editorial discipline.

![annual-report-art-direction-deck methodology illustration](https://cdn1.genspark.ai/user-upload-image/slide_agent/v2-catalog-hero/148-magazine-art-director-annual-report.png)

## Why this skill works

- **The CEO stops asking for more "polish."** Slide 2 declares the stance — Berkshire-substance or Patagonia-polish — so every later debate ("is this too plain?", "is this too glossy?") resolves against a named reference, not against taste.
- **The printer's specs are right in week one.** Trim, paper, ink, finishing, and the financial-page grid are body slides, so production schedules don't slip in week 10 over decisions that should have been made in week 2.
- **You bring the filing target + the stance.** Tell the agent it's a 10-K (substance), an impact report (polish), or an S-1 (somewhere between), name one or two reference reports, and it returns an editorial-system brief.

## Methodology cheat-sheet

**System × Stance × Restraint** — the magazine-AD discipline applied to a 200-page corporate document: one editorial system, one declared stance, two type weights.

1. **System over page** — Pentagram's editorial annual-report work (Public Theater's *Public Speaking*)[^6] treats every spread as part of a system, not a freestanding layout. The grid, the furniture, and the type are inherited; what changes is the content.
2. **Stance: substance or polish, never both** — Berkshire Hathaway's annual letter is the canonical anti-design example[^1]; Patagonia's first-ever *Work in Progress Report* (Nov 2025)[^2][^3] is the canonical editorial-confessional. Both work; mixing them is what fails.
3. **One named photographer or one image discipline** — the AD's signature move. Editorial reports name the photographer in the colophon. Stock mixes are read as junior.
4. **Two type weights, named for the system** — Massimo Vignelli's restraint canon[^9]. Type promiscuity is the most common annual-report error.
5. **The CFO's numbers and the CEO's narrative share one grid** — financial pages aren't a separate document; they share the master grid, just with different furniture (tables, footnote density, smaller measure).
6. **Disclosure language is part of the design** — Pinterest's March 2019 S-1[^4][^5] and the Cooley IPO drafting guide[^8] demonstrate risk-factor sections that are typeset features, not dumped boilerplate. Apple's 10-K[^7] is the opposite-pole reference — minimal photography, restrained type, structural rigor as the brand.

## Before / After

### The design-approach slide

**Typical PPT template**

> **Design Approach**
> - Modern
> - Clean
> - Professional
> - Sustainability-themed
> - On-brand
> - Investor-ready

**This skill's rewrite**

> *(slide 04 — Opening-Spread Concept)*
>
> *(mocked cover: one image, one-word title; opener spread: full-bleed
> photograph captioned with photographer credit; letter spread: 60-char
> measure, 11/15pt Tiempos, accent-red drop cap)*
>
> **Stance: Patagonia confessional.**[^2][^3]
> Photographer Tim Davis on assignment for 4 weeks.

### The "team" slide

**Typical PPT template**

> **Acknowledgments**
> Thanks to everyone who contributed.

**This skill's rewrite**

> **Colophon**
>
> Art Director: <name> · Designers: <name>, <name> · Photographer:
> <name> · Copy Editor: <name> · Printer: <name>, <city> · Paper:
> Mohawk Superfine 80# Text, eggshell · Type: Tiempos Headline /
> Söhne · Cover image: <photographer>, <month> <year>.[^6]

### The financial-pages slide

**Typical PPT template**

> **Financial Performance**
>
> *(8 slides of Excel-grid screenshots, Arial 9pt, blue header bar,
> alternating row shading, no relationship to the editorial section's
> grid, font, or palette)*

**This skill's rewrite**

> **Data-page system**
>
> Same 12-column master grid as the editorial section. Body type:
> Söhne 9/12pt (one weight lighter than the letter spread — Light, not
> Regular, for tabular density). Tabular numerals. Footnote density:
> 8/11pt, drop-shoulder under each table. Accent red `#C8102E` reserved
> for *variance vs prior year* only — never decorative. The CFO's
> tables and the CEO's letter share the same paper.[^6][^7]

## What this skill produces

A 14-slide, 1920×1080 design-system brief for corporate-design directors and IR design partners. The deck is the *brief*, not the report itself — it defines the editorial system, the type, the grid, the colophon, the photo discipline, and the production specs the design team will execute against for the next 11 weeks. The visual mode is ink-and-paper editorial: ink-black body type, paper-white surfaces, one editorial-red accent (Pantone Red 032 — the *Time* / *Economist* lineage), Tiempos / Lyon / GT Sectra display paired with Söhne / Inter body, 12-column grid, 60-72 char measure.
