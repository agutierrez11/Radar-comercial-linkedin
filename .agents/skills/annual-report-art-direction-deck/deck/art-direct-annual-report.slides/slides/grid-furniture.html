RUNNING HEAD · Ch. 02 Risk Factorsp. 31
FX exposure across 41 corridors.
Mirage Pay operates in 41 currency corridors. A 10% adverse move in any of our top-five corridors — BRL, NGN, INR, MXN, IDR — would compress take-rate by ~22 bps on an annualised basis. We hedge ~62% of next-quarter receivables via forwards; the unhedged tail is disclosed quarterly.

Operational FX risk is concentrated in three corridors representing 68% of volume. Settlement timing introduces additional translation risk between book and bank dates.
"~22 bps of take-rate compression at a 10% adverse move."
