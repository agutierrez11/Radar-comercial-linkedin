Letter from the CEO
We built Mirage Pay in 2019 because moving money between two small businesses in different currencies still felt, to us, like sending a fax. Seven years later we settle $84B in annualized volume across 41 corridors, and we still think about that fax.

This document — our prospectus — exists because we are asking the public to fund the next decade of that work. It is structured the way our company is structured: numbers in the back, narrative in the front, risks in plain English in the middle.

— Aisha Okonkwo, co-founder & CEO
