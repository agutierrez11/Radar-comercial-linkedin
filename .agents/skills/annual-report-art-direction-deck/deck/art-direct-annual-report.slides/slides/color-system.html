§ 08 — Color system
Three colors. No cream.
Ink · paper · editorial red. The Time / Economist lineage, in a fintech S-1 register.
01 · Ink
#0E0E0E
Body type on paper. Black-bar callouts. Cover ground when stance is "substance".
02 · Paper
#FFFFFF
Paper ground. No cream, no ivory, no warm beige — that family is the default we are refusing.
03 · Editorial Red
#C8102E
Pantone Red 032. Drop caps, pull-quote rules, eyebrow labels, italics for emphasis. Never as ground for body text.
FORBIDDEN PALETTE. No #F5EFE3, #F7F3EB, #FAF7F2. No "warm bone", no parchment. No dark-serif on cream. That combination is the default we refuse.
Color · 08 / 15 One primary + one accent + neutrals. P0 red line. Editorial-red lineage
