§ 03 — Audience & occasion
Three readers.
Three reading occasions.
Annual reports are read by an audience that already decided to read 200 pages — but the design decides whether they remember anything.
No.ReaderReading occasionWhat they actually openType system speaks toPage weight
01 The buy-side analyst Tuesday, 6:40am, second screen, Bloomberg open. Risk factors, MD&A, cohort tables, footnotes. Body face, 60-char measure, footnote grid. ~45% of pages.
02 The recruit / customer Sunday evening, phone, after reading press coverage. CEO letter, opener spread, photo essay, mission. Display face, full-bleed image, drop cap. ~30% of pages.
03 The journalist Embargo + 2 hours, scanning for the pull-quote. Cover, colophon, one chart, one named statistic. Pull-quotes, captions, callout system. ~25% of pages.
