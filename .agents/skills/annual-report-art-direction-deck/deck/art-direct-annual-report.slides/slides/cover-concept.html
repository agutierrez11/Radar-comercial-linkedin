§ 11 — Cover concept (final)
One image. One line.
The cover is not a poster. It is the magazine's stance, printed twice the size.
Mirage PayForm S-1 · 2026
A ledger,
made
public.
Filed Q3 · NYSE: MIRG · A prospectus for the public listing of Mirage Pay, Inc.
Concept, in one sentence
"The work of going public is the work of opening a ledger that has been closed for seven years."
Stance — Patagonia-class polish. Editorial confessional, named photographer, ink ground for the cover, paper ground for the body.

Hierarchy — The accent word ("ledger") carries the metaphor; everything else is plain English. Type does the rest.

Reader test — A journalist on embargo + 2 hours can lift the title and one image without needing a press kit.
Rejected covers
"Mirage Pay 2026 Annual Report"
— Title is a noun-phrase, not a sentence. Reads as a filing, not a statement.
"Borderless. Boundless."
— Two-word fintech tag. Could belong to any of forty companies.
"The Future of Money"
— Forward-looking, unfounded, and a category we are too small to claim.
"Open." (single word)
— Too thin; gives the cover no metaphor to inherit on Page 1.
